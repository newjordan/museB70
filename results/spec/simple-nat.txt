His poems , many written in the immediate aftermath of war , strongly focused on the human condition , and his style is often praised as the pinnacle of Tang poetry . Many of his poems dealt with social and political issues , and his style and use of language greatly influenced later Chinese poetry . 
 
 = Life = 
 
 Du Fu was born in Gong County , Henan Province , in 712 . His family claimed descent from the Dukes of Zhou . His great @-@ grandfather Du Shenyan was a well @-@ known poet at the time of Emperor Taizong of Tang . His father Du Xun was a minor official . Du Fu was the second son , and had a younger brother Du Zong . He was born in the year of the Water Rabbit , and was given the courtesy name Zimei . 
 Du Fu was a precocious child , and was noted for his literary talent at a young age . He was a student at the Imperial Academy in Chang 'an , the capital of the Tang dynasty .
