 = = Life = = 
 
 Du Fu was born in Gong County , Henan Province , in 712 . His family was of the aristocratic class , claiming descent from the Dukes of Shen . His great @-@ grandfather Du Shenyan was a well @-@ known poet at the court of Emperor Xuanzong of Tang . Du Fu 's father died when he was young , and he was raised by his mother . He was educated in the classics of Confucianism and also studied the works of Qu Yuan . He was a precocious child , and by the age of 15 he had already written a number of poems . 
 In 735 , Du Fu traveled to Chang 'an , the capital of the Tang dynasty , to take the imperial examinations . He failed the examinations , and spent the next few years traveling around China , writing poetry and studying the works of other poets . In 744 , he met Li Bai , who became his close friend and mentor .
