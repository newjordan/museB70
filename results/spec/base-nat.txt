 Du Fu was born in Gong County , Henan Province , in 712 , during the reign of Emperor Xuanzong . His family descended from officials , and his great @-@ grandfather Du Shenyan was a well @-@ known poet of the earlier Tang dynasty . Du Fu 's father died when he was young , and he was raised by his mother . He studied the classics and the works of earlier poets , and he became known for his talent in poetry at a young age . 
 
 Du Fu 's early life was marked by a series of failures in the imperial examinations , which prevented him from obtaining a government position . He traveled extensively throughout China , and his poetry reflected his experiences and observations of the people and the land . He wrote about the beauty of nature , the hardships of war , and the struggles of the common people .
