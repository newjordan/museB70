Meanwhile , Polish university professors and students were arrested and transported to concentration camps in large numbers , often to be exterminated on the spot . The first mass arrests of Polish professors took place in November 1939 as part of the Sonderaktion Krakau , and were followed by mass arrests in Poznań and Warsaw . In 1940 , arrests and executions of Polish professors and scientists were carried out in Lublin , Lwów , Częstochowa , and Kielce , as well as other areas in the General Government . These mass arrests and executions constituted the first stage of Generalplan Ost . 
 The educational system in the General Government was also subject to severe racial stratification . From the very beginning , Jews and ethnic Poles were educated in separate schools , and Jews were not allowed to attend any schools beyond the elementary level . It was not uncommon for Jews to be forbidden from attending even elementary schools . One of the rare exceptions was a short @-@ lived system of Jewish primary schools created in the summer of 1940 at the behest of the General Governor .
