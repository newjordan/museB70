 The treatment of Polish intellectuals and academics by the Nazi occupation was one of the harshest in Europe . The Polish intelligentsia was targeted for total annihilation . On 6 November 1939 , the Nazi German authorities staged a mass arrest of Polish professors and other members of the intelligentsia in the city of Kraków and sent them to concentration camps in Sachsenhausen and Dachau ; this event is referred to as Sonderaktion Krakau . One prominent Polish figure who escaped this fate was Czesław Miłosz , who was forced to move to Warsaw . Similarly , on 3 July 1941 , the Nazi German authorities started the extermination of the intelligentsia in the eastern Polish territories . In 1941 , the Nazi German authorities arrested the entire teaching staff of the University of Warsaw in the so @-@ called " University " or " Professors ' " roundup , and sent many of them to the concentration camps at Auschwitz @-@ Birkenau and Stutthof .
