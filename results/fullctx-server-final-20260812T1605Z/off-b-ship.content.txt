For Polish secondary school pupils , there was a maximum of four hours of lessons per week and , from the spring of 1940 , they were required to perform unpaid labor for two or three days a week . 
 In addition to schools , the Germans destroyed libraries and museums . Libraries were raided , and their collections were either plundered or destroyed . By the end of the German occupation , 75 % of the library resources held by pre @-@ war Polish libraries had been destroyed or stolen . Many of Poland 's museums were closed altogether . The Nazis looted the collections of others , transporting the collections to Germany . The looted art pieces were to be used as decorations for the planned Führermuseum in Linz , or to be used to help make up for the art that had been lost by the Germans in the bombing campaigns . A total of 26 major museums and 132 branch museums were destroyed ; only nine major museums and four branches survived the war in an undamaged or partly damaged state .
