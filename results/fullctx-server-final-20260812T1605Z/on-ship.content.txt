 The Germans allowed only a handful of Polish theaters to operate , including the Polish State Theatre in Warsaw and the Tadeusz Kościuszko Theatre in Łódź . These theaters were permitted to perform only a small number of plays by specific authors , including mainly classics such as those of Szekspir and Molière . All new Polish dramas were banned , as were plays by Polish patriotic authors such as Adam Mickiewicz and Juliusz Słowacki . Even the presentation of these permitted plays was often accompanied by interference on the part of the Nazi censors . Many theaters were shut down in 1940 , and in 1941 , the Polish State Theatre closed because of the Nazi crackdown on the Polish intelligentsia . The Germans attempted to establish a culture of German art and film for Poles by opening numerous cinemas .
